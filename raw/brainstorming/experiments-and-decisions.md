# Experiments and decisions

This is the working register for turning ideas into evidence. No acquisition experiment has been run or funded by this project. The order below is a proposed sequence based on dependencies, not an agreed team schedule.

| Stage | Proposed work | Evidence needed to proceed |
|---|---|---|
| Establish the offer | Resolve fees, available tasks/areas, budget, and existing data. | Answers from City Helpers. |
| Learn from customers | Interviews and booking walkthroughs. | Specific needs, language, and recurring obstacles. |
| Improve booking | Fix the clearest comprehension or completion problem. | Successful repeat walkthroughs. |
| Test acquisition | One community partner/event or one focused Facebook test. | Staff capacity, measurement, and a spending cap. |
| Explore repeat use | A limited referral or reward experiment. | Completed jobs, satisfied customers, and viable economics. |

For each experiment record: problem; audience and task; hypothesis; source; owner; change being tested; budget and staff effort; start/end dates; learning question; customer-growth measure; fulfilment limits; results; and decision with reasons. Mark status as proposed, ready, running, completed, or stopped. Set numerical thresholds with the business before launch.

**Current working decisions:** prioritize seniors booking help themselves; focus initial acquisition on Toronto/GTA; distinguish audience/measurement learning from generating paying customers; organize the knowledge base by business topic. The slide deck is a later expression of this work and does not determine which ideas are worth preserving. These are project decisions from our conversation, not claims of CEO approval.

**Still undecided:** channel priority, budget, partners, incentive mechanics, task focus, and success thresholds. Preserve alternatives and failed ideas with their reasoning rather than silently deleting them.
