# Business context

*Initial public-source snapshot: 22 September 2026. Figures and operating claims need client confirmation.*

[CityHelpers.ca](https://www.cityhelpers.ca/) is a Halifax-based Canadian marketplace connecting seniors, homeowners, and businesses with vetted local workers for short-term jobs. Its origin story centres on founder **Amy Butler-Poulain** seeing how difficult everyday chores became for a friend after a stroke. Household examples include yard care, cleaning, moving, snow removal, and minor repairs; business examples include temporary labour, warehouse work, construction cleanup, and event or restaurant help. The core promise is fast, trustworthy local help and flexible earning opportunities. Its business pitch emphasizes avoiding staffing-agency markups. [About](https://www.cityhelpers.ca/about) · [Business offer](https://www.cityhelpers.ca/for-small-business)

**Model and scale.** The [pricing page](https://www.cityhelpers.ca/pricing-plans/plans-pricing) lists a $9.99 homeowner dispatch fee, $99 business monthly plan, and $999 annual business plan; customers pay workers directly. Worker registration is free, with applications requesting identity, résumé, and video details. [LinkedIn](https://ca.linkedin.com/company/cityhelpers) lists **2–10 employees** and 148 followers: a useful small-team estimate, not a verified headcount. Its listed 2011 founding date conflicts with a [corporate directory’s November 2024 incorporation record](https://www.canadacompanyregistry.com/companies/city-helpers-inc/); confirm the company timeline. Revenue, customer count, worker pool, and transaction volume are unverified.

**Footprint and current approach.** The [contact page](https://www.cityhelpers.ca/contact-3) says Nova Scotia and Ontario are served. Recent LinkedIn posts explicitly promote Halifax and Toronto; Calgary and Edmonton appear as expansion targets, not confirmed active markets. Acquisition appears to combine website signup/request forms, search-oriented service pages, customer testimonials, local business partnerships, and frequent organic LinkedIn posts. A recent LinkedIn recruitment post seeks commission-based B2B sales partners, suggesting outbound membership sales. The company also has identifiable [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61563866975989) and [Nextdoor](https://ca.nextdoor.com/pages/city-helpers-inc/) pages, plus [Indeed worker recruiting](https://ca.indeed.com/viewjob?jk=13896068e2f8dcca). Instagram/TikTok presence and paid-ad spend were not verified. Website copy varies on whether homeowner pricing is per dispatch or monthly and whether City Helpers or the customer pays the worker; clarify before marketing claims.
