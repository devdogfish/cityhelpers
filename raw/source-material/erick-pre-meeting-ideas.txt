1st idea:



May take would be to use all these three groups to advertise CityHelpers through two programs:



CityHelpers Rewards (CHR) and CityHelpers Partners (CHP)



For CHR, customers (individuals or companies) will get CH points for referring the plataform to new subscribers, for using the services and after a certain number of points they cam get perks. Like free services.



These free services would be provided by CH workers that partnered with CH, billing CH only the cost.



For CHP, workers would agree to offer a certain number of services at cost and bill it directly to CH and not the client.



The workers would also earn points based on NPS (net promoting score = great evaluations by customers) plus number of services provided and how many clients they are able to refer to the platform.



Workers would also receive some sort of award or recognition from CH.



On both CHR and CHP the customers and workers would also get point by online reviews (like Google reviews) and posts on social media with specific hashtags.



This is a way to use the users to act as promoters of CH
