Common questions

Who are the helpers?

City Helpers says its workers are local and vetted. Matching considers the job, availability, and location. You can ask City Helpers about its screening process before booking.

What will it cost?

City Helpers lists a dispatch fee and says customers pay workers directly. Review the current pricing page and confirm the worker’s rate before proceeding.

Is help available where I live?

Availability depends on your location and the task. Include your address or neighbourhood when you request help so City Helpers can confirm.
