**Status: mentioned in the meeting; mechanics undefined.** The transcript refers to reward and partner programmes but does not preserve a full explanation. The options below develop that idea and should not be attributed to the team as agreed decisions.

A referral reward would encourage a satisfied customer to introduce a new customer. A repeat-use reward would encourage an existing customer to return. A partner payment would compensate an organization or representative for bringing customers. These address different behaviours and should be evaluated separately; the existing B2B sales recruitment already mentions commissions.

One possible test is a capped dispatch credit earned after a referred customer's first paid, completed job. A second option is a small credit toward a later booking. Values, eligibility, timing, and funding remain undecided. Credits must distinguish the City Helpers fee from worker labour, and the business needs enough contribution per customer to fund them.

**Smallest test:** one incentive with a limited eligible group and a simple referral code. Record issued and redeemed rewards, new paying customers, completed jobs, repeat bookings, and total incentive cost. Compare with an equivalent period or group where possible; reward redemptions alone do not establish additional sales. Check whether customers would have booked anyway and whether the reward creates support work or misuse.
