**Status: team observations and proposed improvements.** Participants described the website as difficult to use and suggested simplifying it. They also proposed customer preference fields and worker skill checkboxes. These observations need a task-based walkthrough; they do not yet quantify abandonment.

First clarify the service, total payment structure, covered jobs, what happens after payment, and how to get help. Reconcile dispatch/subscription language across pages. Then inspect the path from “I need help” through fee payment and submitted request, including mobile use, readability, errors, and recovery after an unfinished request. See the [current journey](customer-journey.html).

Customer preferences could capture task, area, timing, and practical requirements. Collect what is necessary to fulfil the request; additional research questions can be optional or asked later. More profile fields might improve matching but also make booking harder. Worker skill categories could complement résumés, although declared skills still need appropriate verification and availability checks.

**Smallest test:** observe prospective senior customers trying to understand the offer and complete a simulated booking without making a payment. Note confusion, assistance needed, errors, and whether they can explain the fee versus labour cost. Fix the largest recurring obstacle and repeat the task. Assess completion and comprehension before pursuing cosmetic changes or full automation.

Unknowns: analytics, actual checkout behaviour, staff-assisted booking, and matching operations.
