# Business objectives

City Helpers wants more paying customers in Toronto/GTA. Our initial focus is seniors booking household help, reflecting the CEO’s aim to serve “people who want to stay in their home”.

**1. Observability and audience knowledge.** Establish who needs the service, who actually books, where customers come from, and where they abandon the booking process. Use campaign links, referral codes, or “How did you hear about us?” to connect visits, requests, paid dispatches, completed jobs, and repeat bookings by channel. Success here means enough reliable evidence to identify promising audiences and diagnose barriers. We currently lack access to these figures; that does not establish whether City Helpers already collects them.

**2. Customer acquisition.** Use suitable channels, clear messaging, and easier booking to generate more paying customers at a sustainable cost. The CEO stresses that “a marketing campaign is not useful if it sends people to a website that they don't understand.” Success here means additional paid dispatches at a viable cost, supported by completed jobs and repeat bookings. Better measurement supports that outcome, but does not itself deliver customers.

The written hackathon brief requests a 90-day test plan covering audience, channels, messages, website improvements, and estimated results. Our recommendations should specify both what each test will teach us and how it could generate customers, with spending caps, evaluation dates, assumptions, and agreed success measures. The CEO’s transcript supports testing and acquisition work; the explicit 90-day timeframe comes from the brief.

**Proposed measurement:** calculate conversion as paying customers divided by eligible visitors, acquisition cost as attributable spending divided by new paying customers, and completion rate as completed jobs divided by submitted requests. Keep periods and channels comparable; include incentives and staff effort in costs. Track unique customers separately from dispatches. Set targets after confirming baseline data and capacity.
